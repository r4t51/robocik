# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, canvas, and JavaScript. Open `index.html`, or the published GitHub Pages URL on an iPad.

## Users

A child on a phone or iPad who wants a short robot maze they can replay. They also have Pizza u Kotka at `pizza/` and Moja Planeta at `planeta/`.

## Product Purpose

You are a little robot. Jump the walls. Touching a wall sends you back to the start. Red-eyed robots are fast. White-eyed robots are slow. You have three lives. There are 10 labyrinths. After each one you get coins and can buy upgrades.

## Positioning

A pocket maze toy. Not a copy of another game.

## Operating Context

Open the page, tap Graj, walk with the stick, jump walls, reach the green exit. Progress for a run sits in memory for that session. No account.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 10 labyrinths, each harder than the last.
- Jump walls. Wall touch resets to the start of that labyrinth.
- Red eyes chase faster. White eyes wander slower.
- 3 lives. A shop after every labyrinth: extra life, longer jump, faster walk, a shield, slower robots.
- Polish copy.
- Pizza u Kotka stays at `pizza/`. Moja Planeta stays at `planeta/`. The doctor game stays at `lekarz/`.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Robocik.
- You are a robot.
- Ten labyrinths.

## Product Principles

- One loop: walk → jump → exit → shop → next labyrinth.
- Big tap targets. The jump button is obvious.
- A failed jump or a wall touch is fair and instant.
