# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript modules. Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step.

## Users

A child or anyone who wants a short maze game on a phone or a laptop. The first request came from a phone, so the toy has a stick and a jump button as well as keys.

## Product Purpose

You are a small robot. Walk a maze, jump over walls, and reach the green exit before the other robots catch you. Success is finishing three mazes with lives left, or wanting one more try after a wall reset.

## Positioning

A handheld toy on a desk, not a neon cyber arena and not a list of settings.

## Operating Context

The player opens the page, reads four rules, presses Graj, and plays immediately. No account. No score server.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The player is a robot in a maze.
- Jumping clears walls. Touching a wall while on the ground sends the player back to the start.
- Other robots patrol. Red eyes are faster and chase when they can see you. White eyes are slower.
- Touching a robot costs a life. The player starts with 3 lives.
- Three hand-built mazes. Reach the green pad to go on.
- Finishing a maze pays coins. A shop between mazes sells extra life, longer jump, faster walk, a shield, and slower robots.
- Polish language, matching the request.
- Keyboard (arrows / WASD + space) and touch.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Robocik.
- Orange plastic shell, green cutting-mat desk, cardboard walls, teal player, red vs white enemy eyes.

## Evidence on Hand

Rules came from the player: jump walls, wall touch resets, red eyes fast, white eyes slow, robot touch loses a life, three lives.

## Product Principles

- The maze is the toy. Chrome exists to hold lives, jump, and fingers.
- Fairness first: wall hitboxes are inset, jump lasts long enough to clear one wall at walk speed.
- A jump also clears robots, so timing is a reward.
- Copy stays short and in the player's words.
