# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated (inferred; no stack question could be asked): static HTML, CSS, and a small amount of JavaScript. A personal reading site does not need a framework, a build step, or a host-specific runtime. Open `index.html` in a browser.

## Users

Inferred. The primary visitor is someone who wants to know r4t5 through taste: a few books, a few films, a few games, read in one sitting on a phone or a laptop, usually at night. There is no account, no checkout, and no task beyond reading and moving between chapters.

## Product Purpose

A personal site for r4t5. It exists so a visitor can read a short canon: one chapter on books, one on films, one on video games. Success is that the chapters feel written, the three subjects are easy to enter, and the page is unmistakably black and blue.

## Positioning

The site is a private shelf, not a portfolio and not a blog. Each chapter argues why a few works stay, instead of listing scores or streaming links.

## Operating Context

Inferred. The visitor arrives, sees the three chapters, opens one, reads it, and can move to the next without hunting. No login, no CMS, no external accounts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Three chapters: books, films, video games.
- Visual constraint, confirmed by the request: black with blue.
- Language: English, matching the request.
- Public name used on the site: r4t5, taken from the Cursor account display name “r4 t5”. A fuller given name was not confirmed. The account email is not published.
- No contact form, shop, or social feed.

## Brand Commitments

- Name on the site: r4t5.
- Palette commitment: black ground, blue as the second color. No cream, no warm editorial default.

## Evidence on Hand

Confirmed identity is only the handle r4t5. Notion and the workspace contain no verified list of favorite books, films, or games. The titles and the reasons written into the chapters are a synthetic first shelf. A visitor can mistake them for autobiography, so the site labels them as a working shelf in the colophon. Replace them when the real list is known. Do not invent a biography, a city, a job, testimonials, or an email.

## Product Principles

- Chapters are the product. Navigation exists to reach them.
- Specific works over generic taste statements.
- Unconfirmed personal facts stay labeled, never presented as memory.
- The pinned black-and-blue brief outranks category defaults.
- Reading stays possible with motion reduced and on a narrow screen.
