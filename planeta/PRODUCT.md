# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Open `index.html`, or the published GitHub Pages URL on an iPad.

## Users

A child on a phone or iPad who wants to help people on a little planet. They already play Robocik at the site root and Pizza u Kotka at `pizza/`.

## Product Purpose

You are a little person. You pick a name and a look. People on your planet have no homes and no hotel. They are sick. The shop sells medicines and house kits. You give them the right medicine. You also build houses for them. Then they live in the house you built.

## Positioning

A pocket-planet help game: walk, heal, build. It is not an Animal Crossing copy. No Nintendo names, bells, leaf, or their pictures.

## Operating Context

Open the page, write a name, pick a look, walk the planet, buy medicine, heal someone, build them a house. Progress sits in localStorage (`planeta-dom-save`) on that device. No account.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Creator: name, hairstyle, hair color, skin, shirt.
- You walk the planet. There is no hotel.
- Sick people wait outside. They show what hurts.
- The shop sells medicines and house kits (and still a few plants).
- Give the right medicine. They pay. The wrong bottle does nothing.
- Walk to an empty lot and build a house if you have a kit.
- A healed person moves into a house you built. You can walk in and talk.
- Polish copy.
- Pizza u Kotka stays at `pizza/`. Robocik stays at the site root. This planet lives at `planeta/`.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Moja Planeta.
- You are a person, not a cat.
- There is no hotel.
- People are sick and homeless until you help.
- You give medicine and you build houses.

## Product Principles

- One loop: look → shop → give medicine → build house → talk.
- Big tap targets. A sick person and an empty lot are obvious.
- Help comes from you, not from a hotel.
