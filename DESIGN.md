# Design System: Bar Mniam

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Night Window"**

The game is a lunch hatch on a teal evening street. Mint enamel is the stall. Paprika is every action. Lemon is money and the ready state. You stand behind the wood lip and hand plates out.

## Colors

- Night teal owns the street
- Mint enamel owns the stall interior
- Paprika is cook, buy, and go
- Lemon is coins and a customer you can serve now

## Typography

Paytone One on the sign, titles, and fat buttons. Karla on rules and upgrade blurbs.

## Layout

One column on a phone: sign and wallet, then either the creator, the queue-and-stove, or the upgrade list. The recipe dock scrolls sideways.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do

- Draw food and people as shapes, not emoji
- Make a ready match obvious with lemon
- Keep the old maze a quiet text link, not a second hero

### Don't

- Don't turn this into a cream cottage café
- Don't use the Robocik orange handheld shell here
- Don't hide cook and serve behind drag-and-drop
